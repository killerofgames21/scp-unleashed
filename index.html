SCP Unleashed

A YouTube channel that presents articles from the SCP Foundation collaborative fiction wiki as narrated, declassified files.

About the uploader app

"SCP Channel Uploader" is a small private tool run by the channel owner. It builds each video on the owner's own computer and uploads it to the SCP Unleashed YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and has exactly one user: the channel owner.

The app asks Google for permission to upload videos, set thumbnails, add captions, and add videos to playlists on the owner's channel. It does nothing else with the account.

Content and licensing

SCP Foundation articles are written by their respective authors and published on the SCP Wiki under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license. Every video credits the author and links the original article, and the videos are released under the same license.

The SCP Foundation is fiction. Nothing presented on the channel is real.
